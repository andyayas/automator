Privacy

Clinic Admin · last updated 16 October 2026

What this app is

Clinic Admin is a private tool used by one clinic administrator to prepare her daily report, track client packages and insurance approvals, and send appointment reminders. It is not open to the public: sign-ups are closed and only her account can sign in.

What it reads from Google

With her permission, the app reads the clinic's appointment spreadsheet in Google Sheets, read-only (scope spreadsheets.readonly), plus the email address of the Google account she connects, so the app can show which account is connected. It never changes, shares or publishes the spreadsheet.

What it keeps

  • The schedule entries it reads, so it can show the day's bookings and build the daily report.
  • The Google connection (a refresh token), kept on the app's server and never sent to a browser, so the sheet can be read without signing in to Google every hour.

All data is stored in the app's own database, hosted by Supabase in the EU (Frankfurt), and is visible only to the administrator's account.

What it never does

  • It does not sell, share or transfer any data to anyone.
  • It does not use the data for advertising, profiling or training AI models.
  • It reads no other Google data: no Gmail, Drive files, contacts or calendar.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

The connection can be removed at any time in the app (Settings → Google connection → Disconnect), which also revokes it at Google, or from the Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Contact

Questions about this app: use the support email shown on Google's sign-in screen for Clinic Admin.
